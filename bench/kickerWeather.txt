What the weather does to a kick

26465 outdoor kicks and fourth downs from 2015 on, with a recorded temperature and wind.
A fourth down counts as in range from the 43 or closer, and appetite is the share of those where the kicker was sent out.
Each cell is the rate, its standard error and the count. Temperature and wind are nflverse's, rain and snow the Open-Meteo archive's.

Wind make rates are selected: a staff only sends a long one into a gale when they fancy it, so those attempts are the easy ones and the
rate reads high. Read wind through appetite unless the long band says otherwise on its own.
